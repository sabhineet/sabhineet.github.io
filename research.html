Graduate Work
Aug 2025 – Present M.Sc. Thesis

Decoding Disk-Corona-Jet Connections of Stellar-Mass Black Holes through Timing and Spectral Analysis

Supervisor: Dr. Arka Chatterjee, UPES Dehradun  ·  Co-Supervisor: Dr. Sudip K. Garain, IISER Kolkata

This thesis uses X-ray observations from NASA's NuSTAR mission to investigate the physical coupling between the accretion disk, corona, and relativistic jet in stellar-mass black hole systems. The primary observables are light curves, power density spectra, and inter-band time lag spectra extracted from high-energy photon count data.

  • Barycentric correction, source extraction, and light curve production from NuSTAR FITS data
  • Construction of power density spectra (PDS) via Fourier methods to characterise variability
  • Cross-spectral time lag calculations to probe reverberation and propagation delays
  • Spectral modelling to constrain accretion geometry and coronal properties

Aug 2025 – Present External Project

Stochastic Dynamics of Brownian Motion

Collaborator: Dr. Raju Roychowdhury, University of Delhi

This project examines the dynamics of a Brownian particle embedded in a classical non-conservative force field, where standard fluctuation-dissipation relations do not apply in their usual form. The work combines analytical Green's function methods with large-scale Monte Carlo numerical simulation.

  • Derivation and application of Green's functions for the generalised Langevin equation
  • Computation of ensemble-averaged mean-square displacement and velocity autocorrelations
  • Monte Carlo simulation of stochastic trajectories for statistical characterisation
  • Analysis of non-equilibrium steady states and probability distribution evolution

Previous Work
Jun – Jul 2024 Summer Internship · VECC Kolkata

Development of Carbon and Carbon Monoxide Ion Beams for Si Surface Modification and Nanostructuring

Supervisor: Dr. Prasanta Karmakar, Variable Energy Cyclotron Centre

Developed and characterised low-energy ion beams at the VECC ECR Ion Source facility for surface modification of Si(100) substrates. The work involved experimental beam production and theoretical validation via simulation.

  • Optimised C⁺, N⁺, N₂⁺, and CO⁺ beams from the ECR Ion Source
  • Mass separation using dipole magnets and ion implantation into Si(100)
  • SRIM-based simulation of implantation depth profiles for verification
  • Theoretical study of quantum bit device concepts and ion beam control systems

Aug 2023 – May 2024 B.Sc. Thesis

A Comprehensive Study on Detection Techniques and Trends in Characteristics of Exoplanets

Supervisor: Dr. Rashi Jain, UPES Dehradun / University of Southampton

Analysed the NASA Exoplanet Archive to identify systematic trends in planetary properties and to develop a machine learning pipeline capable of predicting unmeasured physical parameters from known observables.

  • Statistical analysis and visualisation of archive data using TOPCAT
  • Identification of detection-technique biases in the published parameter distributions
  • Random forest regression model trained on known planetary properties
  • Model successfully fills measurement gaps introduced by detection method limitations
