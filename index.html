About

I am an M.Sc. Physics graduate from UPES, Dehradun, with a focus on high-energy astrophysics and computational methods.

My graduate thesis examined disk-corona-jet connections in stellar-mass black holes using NuSTAR X-ray data — analysing light curves, time lags, and power density spectra. Concurrently, I collaborated with the University of Delhi on the stochastic dynamics of Brownian motion in non-conservative potentials, combining Green's function analysis with Monte Carlo simulation.

Earlier, a summer internship at the Variable Energy Cyclotron Centre (VECC), Kolkata gave me hands-on experience with ECR ion sources and ion beam implantation on silicon substrates. My undergraduate thesis applied random forest machine learning to the NASA Exoplanet Archive to predict unmeasured planetary properties.

I maintain an open-source Computational Physics Library for students and researchers, and co-founded the Horizon Science Society at UPES.
