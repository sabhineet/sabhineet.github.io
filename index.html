01 / About

Background & Interests

I am an M.Sc. Physics student at UPES, Dehradun, driven by a passion for High Energy Astrophysics and Computational Physics. My research focuses on understanding the extreme environments around stellar-mass black holes.

My work spans X-ray data analysis from the NuSTAR mission, stochastic dynamics simulations using Monte Carlo methods, and machine learning applied to exoplanetary data. I'm equally at home crafting Python pipelines for astronomical archives and deriving analytical solutions with Green's functions.

I co-founded the Horizon Science Society at UPES, organizing workshops with ISRO scientists and field trips to astronomical observatories. I also maintain an open-source Computational Physics Library for students and researchers.
