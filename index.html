01 / About

Background & Interests

I am a Physics graduate with a deep focus on Computational Physics and Numerical Methods, passionate about solving real-world scientific problems through elegant computation.

My academic journey spans classical mechanics, electrodynamics, quantum mechanics, and statistical physics — but my true excitement lives at the intersection of physics and code. I enjoy designing algorithms that simulate physical systems, analyze observational data, and visualize complex phenomena.

Outside research, I contribute to open-source scientific tools, maintain a collaborative computational physics library, and mentor peers in scientific programming.

04 / Research

Research Experience

2023 – 2024

M.Sc. Dissertation

University of Science & Technology · Supervisor: Dr. R. Sharma

Numerical Modelling of Quasi-Periodic Oscillations in Black Hole X-ray Binaries. Developed a Python pipeline for timing analysis of archival RXTE/XMM-Newton data, detecting QPOs in the 1–100 Hz range and constraining inner disc radii through Lense-Thirring precession models.

Astrophysics Timing Analysis Python
2022 – 2023

Research Intern

Institute of Astrophysics · Dr. K. Patel's Group

Contributed to a spectroscopic survey of OB-type stars. Reduced raw echelle spectra, measured radial velocities, and identified chemical peculiarities in a sample of 40 stellar spectra using automated line-profile fitting routines written in Python.

Spectroscopy Data Reduction SciPy
2021 – 2022

Undergraduate Project

National University · Computational Physics Lab

Implemented finite-difference solvers for the 2D heat equation and Schrödinger equation in an infinite square well, comparing numerical eigenvalues to analytical solutions and visualising probability density evolution.
