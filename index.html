About

I am an M.Sc. Physics graduate student at UPES, Dehradun, focusing on high-energy astrophysics and computational methods.

My graduate thesis examines disk-corona-jet connections in stellar-mass black holes using NuSTAR X-ray data — analysing light curves, time lags, and power density spectra to probe the physical geometry of accretion systems. Concurrently, I collaborate with the University of Delhi on Brownian motion in non-conservative potentials, combining Green's function analysis with Monte Carlo simulation.

A summer internship at the Variable Energy Cyclotron Centre (VECC), Kolkata gave me hands-on experience with ECR ion sources and ion beam implantation on silicon substrates. My undergraduate thesis applied random forest machine learning to the NASA Exoplanet Archive to predict unmeasured planetary properties.

I maintain an open-source Computational Physics Library for students and researchers, and co-founded the Horizon Science Society at UPES. I have also completed the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate (9-course specialisation), building skills in SQL, R, Tableau, and data storytelling.
