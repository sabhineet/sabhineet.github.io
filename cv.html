02 Academic Projects

M.Sc. Thesis — UPES, Dehradun

Aug 2025 – Present

Decoding Disk-Corona-Jet Connections of Stellar Mass Black Holes through Timing and Spectral Analysis

Supervisors: Dr. Arka Chatterjee (UPES) · Dr. Sudip K. Garain (IISER Kolkata)

  • Utilised X-ray data from the NuSTAR mission to study stellar-mass black holes
  • Analysed light curves, time lags, and power density spectra from observed variability
  • Investigating disk-corona-jet coupling in stellar-mass black hole systems

Stochastic Dynamics of Brownian Motion

Collaborator: Dr. Raju Roychowdhury (University of Delhi)

  • Studying Brownian motion dynamics in classical non-conservative systems
  • Applying Green's functions to analyse analytical behaviour and correlations
  • Monte Carlo simulations for stochastic modelling and statistical analysis

Summer Internship — VECC, Kolkata

Jun – Jul 2024

Development of Carbon and Carbon Monoxide Ion Beams for Si Surface Modification and Nanostructuring

Supervisor: Dr. Prasanta Karmakar (Variable Energy Cyclotron Centre)

  • Developed and optimised low-energy ion beams (C⁺, N⁺, N₂⁺, CO⁺) using an ECR Ion Source
  • Mass separation with dipole magnets and ion implantation on Si(100) substrate
  • Verified through simulated implantation using SRIM
  • Theoretical study of quantum computing devices and ion beam control systems

Aug 2023 – May 2024

A Comprehensive Study on Detection Techniques and Trends in Characteristics of Exoplanets

Supervisor: Dr. Rashi Jain (UPES / University of Southampton)
